# Brighten & Renew Facial - Target Market Research

## Service Details
- **Treatment:** Professional Dermaplaning + Chemical Peel combo
- **Price:** £100
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Results last:** 2-4 weeks
- **Recommended frequency:** Every 3-4 weeks

## Target Audience

### Demographics
- Women 30-55 (sweet spot 35-50)
- Middle-class professionals, £40K-£80K+ household income
- Southampton/Hampshire area
- Office professionals, managers, healthcare workers, teachers, stay-at-home mums
- Married/partnered, may have kids
- Willing to spend £100 on quality skincare/treatments

### Psychographics
- Researches obsessively before buying (reads reviews, watches videos)
- Values clean beauty and professional appearance
- Has tried multiple skincare products (Drunk Elephant, The Ordinary, CeraVe, Sephora)
- Frustrated nothing is working despite effort and money spent
- Scared of Botox/needles but wants visible results
- Partner doesn't notice her skincare efforts

## Painful Current State

### Fears
- Looking older than they feel
- Scalpel cutting them or leaving scars
- Hair growing back thicker/darker (common myth)
- Wasting £100 on something that doesn't work
- Pain or discomfort
- Looking "done" or unnatural

### Daily Frustrations
- Skin looks dull and tired even after 8 hours of sleep
- Makeup sits on top of skin instead of blending (cakey, patchy)
- Visible peach fuzz that catches light
- Foundation clinging to dry patches and rough texture
- Pores looking huge and noticeable
- Products not absorbing properly
- Fine lines becoming more visible every month

### Embarrassments
- Visible facial hair (peach fuzz) in photos
- Skin texture that looks rough up close
- Looking tired when they're not
- Needing more makeup to look "normal" than they used to
- Comparing themselves to other women who seem to glow effortlessly
- Friends/colleagues looking younger despite being the same age

### Emotional State
- Frustrated and defeated (trying everything and nothing works)
- Self-conscious in social situations and photos
- Less confident at work and relationships
- "You look tired" (most hated comment)
- Feel like they're losing control of their appearance
- Partners don't notice their efforts with skincare

### Barriers to Action
- Don't know dermaplaning + chemical peel combo exists
- Scared of "scalpel" treatments
- Think professional treatments are too expensive
- Tried drugstore products that failed and lost hope
- Don't want needles/invasive treatments
- Overwhelmed by skincare options and contradictory advice
- Fear of looking "done" or unnatural

### What They Want
- Wake up with smooth, glowing, radiant skin (no makeup needed)
- Makeup glides on effortlessly and looks flawless all day
- Skin feels like silk, no rough patches or peach fuzz
- Look 5-10 years younger without anyone knowing they "had work done"
- A natural glow that makes people ask "what's your secret?"
- Confident in photos, videos, and bright lighting
- Partner finally noticing

### Secret Desires
- To be the woman who "looks amazing for her age"
- Compliments on their skin
- To feel beautiful without heavy makeup
- People asking "what do you use on your skin?"
- To age gracefully while still looking younger than their years

### Who They Want to Impress
- Their partner/husband
- Themselves
- Friends and colleagues
- Other women (subtle competition)
- Anyone taking their photo

## How They Evaluate Solutions
- Before/after photos (most important)
- Reviews and testimonials from real people
- Immediate visible results
- Professional vs at-home distinction
- Science-backed ingredients and explanations
- Recommendations from trusted friends
- Price: not too cheap (seems dodgy), not too expensive

## Brands They Respect
- Drunk Elephant (clean beauty)
- The Ordinary (affordable, science-based, transparent)
- CeraVe (dermatologist-recommended)
- Dr. Dennis Gross (professional-grade peels)
- Estheticians and dermatologists over influencers
- Real people with before/afters over celebrities
