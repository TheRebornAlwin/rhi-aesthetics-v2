# Skin Lesion Removal - Target Market Research

## Demographics
- **Gender**: Women (80/20)
- **Age**: 30-60
- **Occupation**: 9-5ers, office workers, retail workers, healthcare workers
- **Income**: £2,000-£3,000/month (has disposable income but price-sensitive)
- **Location**: Southampton

## Painful Current State

### Fears
- Treatment will hurt
- Healing process (will there be scarring?)
- People staring at their moles, warts, etc.
- If the mole can even be removed
- When speaking, people look at the mole, not them
- Price being too high and unaffordable
- Picking the wrong aesthetician/dermatologist
- It might be cancerous
- Infection after treatment
- Downtime before returning to work, wearing makeup, etc.

### Anger
- The mole/wart being visible
- NHS stopping this service, forcing private payment
- Aging
- Other clinics charging £500+

### Daily Frustrations
- Worrying/stressing about mole or wart
- Trying to cover it or hide that side of face
- Seeing it every morning in the mirror
- Wearing long sleeves or high necks to hide it
- Mole catching on clothes, jewelry, bracelets
- Applying heavier makeup to cover it

### Embarrassment
- Speaking to people who look at the mole
- Mole being visible on their face
- Filming or taking pictures showing the mole
- Family pictures where mole/wart is clearly visible
- People noticing the mole is gone after treatment

### Self-Feelings
- Anxious, scared, nervous before treatment
- Slightly insecure during treatment
- Frustrated
- Helpless

### Others' Perceptions
- May be made fun of or taken advantage of due to insecurities

### What They'd Say to Friends
"I hate my mole and the way it looks. It makes me feel insecure and I feel like people are always staring at it, especially when I'm speaking to them - it really puts me off."

### Current Barriers
- Scared of healing process and potential scarring
- Think treatment will be painful and uncomfortable
- Worried skin might get discolored where mole was removed
- **PRICE IS A BIG THING**
- Don't know where to go (NHS not an option)
- Don't know if mole can even be removed

## Market Sophistication Level

**LEVEL 4 MARKET**

### Why Level 4:
- Everyone offers same removal methods (laser, surgical excision, cryotherapy) - mechanism is no longer unique
- All clinics make identical claims ("painless," "no scarring," "quick procedure") - claims are tired and don't work
- Customers comparing versions of same thing: "Which laser clinic? Which doctor? Which method is ACTUALLY better?"
- Market is jaded but still buying - they want mole removal, they just don't know which clinic's version is best

### How to Stand Out in Level 4:

Position Rhi's version of mole removal as BETTER through:

1. **Ongoing Healing Support** - "Day-by-day healing guidance + WhatsApp access during recovery" (competitors just remove and forget)
2. **Radical Transparency** - Show ACTUAL healing process with real photos (Day 1, 3, 7, Week 2) - not just before/after
3. **Human Connection** - Lead with Rhi's face, personality, empathy ("I get it - you've been covering this for years") - not sterile clinic vibes
4. **Two-Path CTA** - "Book consultation" OR "Message Rhi first with questions" (removes pressure, builds trust before commitment)
5. **Guarantee** - "If you're not happy with the result, we'll make it right" (competitors don't offer this)
6. **Personalized Experience** - "Free consultation where I assess YOUR mole and explain YOUR options" (not generic "we remove moles")

## Top Player Analysis (TPA)

### Top Competitors:
1. Excel Clinic - https://excelclinic.co.uk/minor-surgery/
2. Wall Street Dermatology - https://www.wallstreetdermatology.com/mole-removal/
3. CLNQ - https://www.clnq.com/skin-treatments/laser-mole-removal/

### What Top Players Do Well:

1. **Medical Positioning** - Position as medical/clinical (not beauty salons): "Minor Surgery," "Dermatology," "Medical-grade"
2. **Clear Credentials** - Doctors, surgeons, CQC registered, medical degrees
3. **Process Explanation** - Consultation → Assessment → Removal → Aftercare
4. **Specific Methods** - List removal methods: Shave excision, surgical excision, cryotherapy, laser
5. **Address Cancer Fear** - Mole checks, histology testing, skin cancer screening
6. **Show Pricing** - Excel: from £250, Wall Street: $395-$795, CLNQ: from £295
7. **Same-Day Removal** - "Walk in with a mole, walk out without it"
8. **Emphasize Comfort** - Local anaesthetic, quick procedure (15-30 mins)
9. **Multiple Locations** - London locations, multiple clinics
10. **Before/After Photos** - CLNQ and Wall Street show results
11. **Free Consultations** - Low-pressure way to assess
12. **Fast Healing** - "Back to normal activities immediately," "minimal scarring"
13. **Trust Badges** - Medical associations, years in practice, number of procedures performed

### What Top Players Are NOT Doing Well (Opportunities):

1. **Websites feel sterile and clinical** - No warmth, no human connection, very "medical office" vibe
2. **No practitioner personality** - You don't "meet" the doctor, just generic "our team" language
3. **Before/afters are limited or generic** - Not enough, not labeled by area (face vs body vs specific mole types)
4. **No healing timeline shown** - They say "minimal scarring" but don't show day-by-day what to expect
5. **No addressing emotional pain** - They don't speak to the insecurity, embarrassment, daily frustration
6. **Copy is very technical** - "Melanocytic nevi," "histopathology," "excisional biopsy" (scary for average person)
7. **No "who this is for" qualification** - They don't help people self-assess if they're a good candidate
8. **No patient stories or testimonials with depth** - Just star ratings, no actual narratives
9. **Don't address "NHS vs private" confusion** - Why can't I get this on NHS? Why do I have to pay?
10. **No transparency on downtime** - "Minimal" isn't specific enough (can I wear makeup tomorrow? go to work?)
11. **CTAs are generic** - "Book Consultation" without explaining what happens in the consultation
12. **No FAQ addressing real fears** - Will it hurt? Will it scar? What if it grows back?
13. **Hero images are stock photos or generic clinic shots** - Not real patients, not real results
14. **No addressing price anxiety** - They list prices but don't justify them or explain why it's worth it
15. **No "what NOT to do" education** - They don't warn against DIY removal or explain why professionals are safer

### What Makes Them Top Dogs:

1. **Medical credibility** - Doctors, not beauticians = trust
2. **Remove cancer fear** - Offer mole checks and histology = peace of mind
3. **Convenient** - Same-day removal, multiple locations, walk-in-walk-out
4. **Look established** - Professional sites, years in business, thousands of procedures
5. **Offer choice** - Multiple removal methods based on mole type
6. **Transparent on process** - You know what will happen step-by-step
7. **Emphasize safety** - Local anaesthetic, sterile environment, medical-grade equipment
