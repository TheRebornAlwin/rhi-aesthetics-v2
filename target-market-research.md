# Skin Lesion Removal - Target Market Research

## Demographics
- **Gender**: Women (80/20)
- **Age**: 30-60
- **Occupation**: 9-5ers, office workers, retail workers, healthcare workers
- **Income**: £2,000-£3,000/month (has disposable income but price-sensitive)
- **Location**: Southampton

## Painful Current State

### Fears
- Treatment will hurt
- Healing process (will there be scarring?)
- People staring at their moles, warts, etc.
- If the mole can even be removed
- When speaking, people look at the mole, not them
- Price being too high and unaffordable
- Picking the wrong aesthetician/dermatologist
- It might be cancerous
- Infection after treatment
- Downtime before returning to work, wearing makeup, etc.

### Anger
- The mole/wart being visible
- NHS stopping this service, forcing private payment
- Aging
- Other clinics charging £500+

### Daily Frustrations
- Worrying/stressing about mole or wart
- Trying to cover it or hide that side of face
- Seeing it every morning in the mirror
- Wearing long sleeves or high necks to hide it
- Mole catching on clothes, jewelry, bracelets
- Applying heavier makeup to cover it

### Embarrassment
- Speaking to people who look at the mole
- Mole being visible on their face
- Filming or taking pictures showing the mole
- Family pictures where mole/wart is clearly visible
- People noticing the mole is gone after treatment

### Self-Feelings
- Anxious, scared, nervous before treatment
- Slightly insecure during treatment
- Frustrated
- Helpless

### Others' Perceptions
- May be made fun of or taken advantage of due to insecurities

### What They'd Say to Friends
"I hate my mole and the way it looks. It makes me feel insecure and I feel like people are always staring at it, especially when I'm speaking to them - it really puts me off."

### Current Barriers
- Scared of healing process and potential scarring
- Think treatment will be painful and uncomfortable
- Worried skin might get discolored where mole was removed
- **PRICE IS A BIG THING**
- Don't know where to go (NHS not an option)
- Don't know if mole can even be removed

## Desirable Dream State

### Magic Wand Vision
- Way more confident in themselves and their skin
- Moles/warts would be gone
- Zero to little scarring or skin discoloration
- Able to take photos freely without angling
- Living stress-free

### Who They Want to Impress
- Family and friends after treatment
- Boyfriends or husbands
- Themselves above all (to feel more confident)
- Colleagues at work

### Dream State Feelings
- Confident in appearance/face
- Sense of stress relief
- Proud of themselves

### Secret Desires
- To feel way more confident and no longer worried about their mole/wart

### What They'd Say to Friends After
"I feel so much more confident in myself and feel like I don't have to cover up or hide my face or mole anymore. I can actually speak to people without thinking they're just staring at my mole. I wish I got this done sooner."

## Values, Beliefs & Tribal Affiliations

### Current Beliefs
- Skin lesion removal is expensive
- Mole removal will be painful
- Scarring is a big possibility
- They feel less confident due to the mole
- NHS won't help unless it's cancerous
- They're stuck with it

### Who They Blame
- Haters or people who stare and laugh at their mole (makes them more insecure)
- Their genetics
- Aging
- The NHS

### Past Solution Attempts
- Most likely never tried to solve it
- If tried: creams to fade the mole (didn't work - creams can't remove lesions)

### Solution Evaluation Criteria
- **MUST SEE**: Plenty of social proof (people like them with successful results)
- Treatment isn't painful or uncomfortable
- Practitioner credentials
- Before/after photos
- Clear pricing
- Consultation process
- Aftercare support

### Respected Figures/Brands
- Established clinics with years of experience
- Clinics specializing in dermatology/skin concerns
- Clinics showing real before/afters

### Valued Character Traits
- Honesty
- Kind and caring
- Happy and positive
- Professionalism
- Empathy
- Competence

### Despised Character Traits
- Untrustworthy
- Dishonest
- Pushy/salesy
- Unprofessional

### Market Trends Awareness
- NHS no longer covers cosmetic mole removal
- Social media influencers getting cosmetic procedures

### Tribal Affiliations
- "Self-improvement" tribe

## Intro Offer Strategy
**£49 for 1-2 Small Lesion Removal**
- Removes price barrier
- Lets them test skill level
- Experience pain level
- See healing process
- Check for scarring
- Builds trust
- Removes fear of commitment
