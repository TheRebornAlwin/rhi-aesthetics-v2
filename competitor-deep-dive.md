# Competitor Deep Dive Analysis - Mole Removal Websites

**Analysis Date:** January 8, 2026
**Purpose:** Comprehensive competitive analysis of mole removal landing pages
**Total Sites Analyzed:** 3 (2 successful, 1 offline)

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines three competitor websites in the mole/skin lesion removal space. Two sites were successfully analyzed (Excel Clinic UK and Wall Street Dermatology NYC), while CLNQ was offline during analysis. Key findings:

- **Pricing transparency varies dramatically** - Excel Clinic shows all prices upfront, Wall Street hides pricing
- **Credential positioning is critical** - Wall Street heavily features Dr. Tzu's credentials and stats
- **Before/after galleries** - Only Wall Street has them (11 images in slider format)
- **Tone spectrum** - Excel is clinical/transactional, Wall Street is warm/premium/reassuring

**Rating: 8/10 Clinical, 2/10 Warm**

**Overall:** This is a very clinical, transactional approach. Feels like a medical factory optimized for volume and efficiency. Works well for price-conscious, no-nonsense customers who want clarity and speed. Would NOT appeal to anxious patients seeking reassurance or those wanting a premium, personalized experience.

### Pricing Strategy

**NO PRICING shown anywhere on the page.**

One mention: "Book an appointment for cosmetic mole removal in NYC to discuss the best approach, pricing, and what to expect from your procedure."

**Analysis:** This is deliberate premium positioning. By hiding pricing, they:
1. Force consultation bookings
2. Avoid price-shopping
3. Signal premium/bespoke service
4. Can price based on complexity during consultation

This works because Dr. Tzu's credentials and results justify premium pricing. Opposite strategy from Excel Clinic.
